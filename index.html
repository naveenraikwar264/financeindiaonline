Personal Finance Basics

Useful topics for salaried people, small business owners and families.

Budget

Monthly Budget

Track income and expenses. Keep needs, wants and savings separate. A simple budget helps you avoid unnecessary debt.

Savings

Emergency Fund

Keep 3 to 6 months of basic expenses in safe and liquid options before taking higher investment risk.

Loans

EMI Planning

Before taking a loan, compare interest rate, processing fee, tenure, prepayment rules and total repayment amount.

Insurance

Risk Protection

Health insurance and term insurance protect your family from sudden financial pressure. Insurance is not the same as investment.

Investment

Long-Term Growth

Understand risk, time horizon and diversification before investing. Avoid schemes promising guaranteed double money.

Tax

Tax Awareness

Maintain documents for income, deductions, insurance, investments and business expenses. Consult a qualified tax professional when needed.

Money Checklist

Use this as a starting point for personal financial planning.

Step 1

Write your monthly income, fixed expenses, variable expenses and debt EMIs.

Step 2

Create an emergency fund and avoid using credit cards for unnecessary purchases.

Step 3

Review insurance, tax documents and investment goals at least once every year.
